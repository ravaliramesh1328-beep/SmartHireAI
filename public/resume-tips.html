📄 Resume Tips

How to Create a Good Resume

  • ✅ Keep your resume to 1–2 pages.
  • ✅ Use a professional format.
  • ✅ Add your full name and contact details.
  • ✅ Write a short professional summary.
  • ✅ List your education and qualifications.
  • ✅ Highlight your technical and soft skills.
  • ✅ Mention your work experience or internships.
  • ✅ Include relevant projects and achievements.
  • ✅ Add certifications if available.
  • ✅ Check for spelling and grammar mistakes.

Resume Sections

  1. 👤 Personal Information
  2. 🎯 Career Objective
  3. 🎓 Education
  4. 💻 Technical Skills
  5. 💼 Work Experience
  6. 📂 Projects
  7. 🏆 Certifications
  8. 🌐 Languages Known
  9. 📞 Contact Information

Common Resume Mistakes

  • ❌ Spelling mistakes
  • ❌ Wrong contact details
  • ❌ Too much unnecessary information
  • ❌ Poor formatting
  • ❌ Using an unprofessional email address
