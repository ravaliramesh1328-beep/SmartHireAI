🗣️ Communication Skills

Why Communication Skills Matter

Good communication helps you express your ideas clearly, answer interview questions confidently, and create a positive impression on recruiters.

Tips to Improve Communication

  • ✅ Speak clearly and confidently.
  • ✅ Maintain eye contact.
  • ✅ Listen carefully before answering.
  • ✅ Avoid filler words like "um" and "ah".
  • ✅ Keep your answers simple and structured.
  • ✅ Practice speaking in English every day.
  • ✅ Improve your vocabulary.
  • ✅ Smile and stay positive.

Interview Communication Tips

  1. 👋 Greet the interviewer politely.
  2. 😊 Introduce yourself confidently.
  3. 🎯 Answer only what is asked.
  4. 💡 Give examples from your experience.
  5. 🙏 Thank the interviewer before leaving.

Daily Practice Ideas

  • 📖 Read English newspapers or articles.
  • 🎤 Practice speaking in front of a mirror.
  • 🎥 Watch English interview videos.
  • 🗨️ Talk with friends in English.
  • 📝 Learn 5 new words every day.
