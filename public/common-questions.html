❓ Common Interview Questions

  1. Tell me about yourself.

    Introduce yourself with your education, skills, projects, and career goals.

  2. Why do you want to join our company?

    Explain what you know about the company and why you are interested in working there.

  3. Why should we hire you?

    Highlight your strengths, skills, and how you can contribute to the company.

  4. What are your strengths?

    Example: Quick learner, teamwork, problem-solving, communication skills.

  5. What are your weaknesses?

    Mention a real weakness and explain how you are improving it.

  6. Describe a challenging situation you handled.

    Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result).

  7. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

    Talk about career growth, learning, and taking more responsibilities.

  8. Are you willing to relocate?

    Answer honestly based on your preference.

  9. How do you handle pressure?

    Explain how you stay calm, prioritize tasks, and solve problems.

  10. Do you have any questions for us?

    Ask about the role, team, training, or growth opportunities.

✅ Interview Tips

  • ✔ Be confident and polite.
  • ✔ Listen carefully before answering.
  • ✔ Keep answers clear and concise.
  • ✔ Give examples from your experience.
  • ✔ Thank the interviewer at the end.
